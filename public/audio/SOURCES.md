# MTTClock audio

- pacer-beep.wav: original synthesized 880 Hz chime with a quiet second harmonic, generated mathematically for MTTClock. No sampled recording. The filename is retained for compatibility.
- one-minute.wav and level-end.wav: generated Windows speech synthesis of the short phrases “One minute remaining this level” and “End of this level.”

No FitnessGram recording, narration, music, or private uploaded images are included.
